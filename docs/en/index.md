# Parallaxe

<p class="px-tagline">See beyond your files. A free, open source, local-first library to organize, inspect and preview your creative assets — without moving them, without an account, without the cloud.</p>

[Read the vision](project/vision.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
[Contribute](contributing/index.md){ .md-button }

</div>

!!! warning "Early design phase"
    Parallaxe is not usable yet. This site documents what is being built and why.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-folder-eye-outline: **Your folders, untouched**

    ---

    Parallaxe references the folders you already have. Tags and collections are virtual: originals are never moved, renamed or rewritten.

-   :material-cube-outline: **3D at the core**

    ---

    Interactive GLB/glTF, OBJ and STL viewer with triangles, vertices, materials, textures, animations and missing references.

-   :material-shield-lock-outline: **Local-first, no account**

    ---

    Works offline. Nothing is uploaded. No telemetry, no login, no subscription.

-   :material-scale-balance: **Truthful metadata**

    ---

    Every technical value says how it was computed. Unknown is shown as unknown, never as `0`.

</div>
